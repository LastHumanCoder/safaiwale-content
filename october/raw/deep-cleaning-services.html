What does a deep clean include that a regular clean does not?

A regular clean keeps visible surfaces tidy. A deep clean goes after build-up in the places nobody reaches week to week. The difference shows up most in the kitchen and bathrooms.

What are the types of deep cleaning services?

The types of deep cleaning services differ by the state of the home and which rooms need the most work.

1. Occupied home deep clean

The most common job: a lived-in home that needs a full reset, often before a festival, a family event or after the monsoon. The crew works around your furniture and belongings.

2. Move-in and move-out deep clean

An empty home is faster to clean and lets the crew reach every cabinet, wardrobe and shelf inside and out. This is the best time to do a thorough clean of a rented or newly bought flat.

3. Post-renovation deep clean

Cement dust, paint drips and tile adhesive need scraping and repeated wiping. Share photos before booking, because heavy construction residue may be quoted above the standard slab.

4. Kitchen deep clean

For homes where only the kitchen has built up grease. See our kitchen cleaning service for this standalone option.

5. Bathroom deep clean

A single bathroom deep clean is ₹499, useful when hard water scale is the main problem.

6. Villa deep clean

Multi-floor homes with staircases, terraces, more bathrooms and larger glass areas. Villas have their own slab because of the extra area and movement between floors.

How do you know your home is due? A sticky film on kitchen cabinet handles, white scale rings around bathroom taps, grey lines on fan blades and a musty smell in wardrobes after the rains all point to build-up that a weekly mop will not shift.

How does the deep cleaning process work?

  1. Quote. You share your home size, condition, city and date. We confirm the slab price and any add-ons.
  2. Preparation. Clear countertops, put away valuables, and empty cabinets or the fridge if you want their interiors done.
  3. Wet areas first. The crew starts with the kitchen and bathrooms, since degreasers and descalers need time to work.
  4. Top to bottom. Fans, lights and high shelves are cleaned before furniture, and furniture before floors, so dust never lands on a clean surface.
  5. Floors last. Floors are vacuumed, scrubbed and mopped once everything above them is done.
  6. Final walk-through. You check each room against the checklist with the crew before they leave.

Safety notes for the crew and for you: the crew never mixes bleach with acid or ammonia products, tests new products on a hidden patch first, and keeps rooms ventilated while strong cleaners are in use. Switch off and unplug any appliance you want cleaned.

What should you check before booking a deep clean?

  • Does the quote list the chimney, grout and fans? These are the deep clean jobs most often left out of a cheap quote.
  • Will the crew bring a scrubbing machine and ladders? Without them, floors and high areas get a surface wipe.
  • Are cabinet and fridge interiors included? Agree on this before the day so you can empty them in advance.
  • Are products safe for your surfaces? Marble and natural stone need pH-neutral cleaners; acid descalers damage them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on home size and condition. A 1 or 2 BHK usually takes most of a working day, while a 4 BHK takes six to eight hours with a larger crew. Villas and post-renovation homes can take longer, and we tell you the expected time when we confirm the booking.

Should I book pest control before or after a deep clean?

Book pest control first, then the deep clean after the technician says treated areas can be wiped. Cleaning first and treating afterwards means the crew cannot clean the treated cracks and corners without removing the product. For gel treatments, ask the technician which spots to leave untouched.

Do you clean inside wardrobes and kitchen cabinets?

Yes, when they are empty. In a move-in or move-out deep clean the crew wipes interiors as standard. In an occupied home, empty the cabinets you want done before the crew arrives and mention them when you book so time is planned for them.

Is deep cleaning safe for marble and wooden floors?

Yes, as long as the crew uses the right product. Marble, Kota and granite need pH-neutral cleaners, since acid and bleach etch and dull stone. Wooden floors need a damp, not wet, mop. Tell us your floor types when you book so the crew brings the correct products.

Can I get only the kitchen and bathrooms deep cleaned?

Yes. A bathroom deep clean is ₹499 per bathroom, and the kitchen can be booked as a standalone kitchen deep clean. Combining both with a full home deep clean usually works out better value if the rest of the home also needs attention.
