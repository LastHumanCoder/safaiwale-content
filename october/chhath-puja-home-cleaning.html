Chhath Puja Home Cleaning: Kitchen and Ghat Hygiene Guide

Chhath Puja home cleaning is best done in two passes: a full deep clean folded into your Diwali cleaning, then a focused clean of the kitchen, prasad area and puja corner in the few days between Diwali and the first day of Chhath. Splitting Chhath Puja home cleaning this way matters because the gap is short. Diwali falls on Sunday 8 November in 2026 and Chhath is in mid-November, around 13 to 16 November in 2026; confirm the dates for your city. Good Chhath Puja home cleaning centres on the kitchen, because that is where thekua and the other prasad are made with extra care for purity. This guide treats Chhath Puja home cleaning as a hygiene job: a timeline, a kitchen checklist, prasad-area habits, pest control and ghat hygiene. Customs vary between families and regions, so follow the elders in your home on every ritual matter.

When should house cleaning before Chhath Puja start?

Start the heavy work in late October, alongside your Diwali clean, and keep the days after Diwali for the kitchen and prasad area only. Trying to deep clean the whole house after Diwali leaves you scrubbing cracker residue and sweet boxes at the same time as preparing for the fast.

How the four days shape your cleaning

Chhath is dedicated to the Sun God and Chhathi Maiya and is observed over four days. In general terms, the first day (Nahay Khay) begins with a bath and a simple satvik meal, the second (Kharna) is a day of fasting that ends with an evening prasad, the third is the evening offering at the water to the setting sun, and the fourth is the dawn offering to the rising sun, after which the fast ends. Practices differ from family to family.

For cleaning, this means the kitchen has to be ready before the first day and stay clean through the second and third, when most of the cooking happens.

Chhath Puja kitchen cleaning, step by step

  1. Empty the shelves you will use. Move everyday spices, onion, garlic and non-vegetarian items to a separate cupboard or another room, as many families keep these away during the festival. Wipe the empty shelves with mild dish soap and dry them.
  2. Degrease the hob and burners. Turn off the gas supply and let the stove cool. Soak the burners in hot soapy water, scrub the ports with an old toothbrush and dry them fully before relighting.
  3. Wash the chimney filters. Switch off the chimney at the mains, soak metal filters in hot water with dish soap and baking soda, rinse and dry. Frying thekua puts a lot of oil into the air, and a clogged filter drips it back down.
  4. Scrub the sink and drain. Clear the drain strainer, pour hot water down, and scrub the sink with baking soda.
  5. Clean the fridge. Throw out leftovers, wash the shelves in warm soapy water and dry them, so fruit and milk for the offerings go into a clean space.
  6. Wash walls, switches and handles. Grease collects on switchboards and cabinet handles near the stove. Wipe them with a barely damp soapy cloth, keeping water away from sockets.
  7. Finish with the floor. Sweep, then mop twice: once with a detergent solution to lift grease, once with clean water.

Never mix bleach with ammonia or acidic cleaners while you work, and keep a window open.

Chhath Puja prasad kitchen hygiene

Prasad for Chhath is prepared with great care, and many households follow strict rules about who cooks, what is used and how. Leave those decisions to your family. The practical hygiene below supports whatever your custom is.

Utensils and cooking area

Many families use a separate set of utensils for Chhath, or buy new ones. Wash them in fresh water with a mild soap, rinse twice and dry them in the sun or with a clean cotton cloth kept only for these utensils. Store them covered in a clean cupboard until the cooking day. If your family uses a separate stove or a clay chulha, set it up on a swept, washed area well away from curtains, and keep a bucket of water nearby.

Wheat, jaggery and ghee

If your family washes wheat at home and dries it before milling, spread it on a clean sheet on a swept roof or balcony, cover it with a net against birds and dust, and let it dry completely. Damp grain grows mould and spoils in storage. Keep jaggery and sugar in airtight jars, because they draw ants within hours. Buy ghee and oil in sealed packs and check the dates.

Cooking and storing thekua

Wash your hands before each cooking session, tie long hair back and keep pets out of the kitchen while prasad is being made. Let thekua cool completely on a clean plate before storing it, then keep it in clean, dry, airtight steel tins. Putting it away warm traps steam, and soft thekua spoils faster. Fruit for the soop should be washed in clean water and dried before arranging.

Soop and daura

Bamboo soop and daura are often bought new each year. If you reuse one, dust it with a dry brush and leave it in the sun for a few hours. Do not soak bamboo, because it holds moisture and grows mould inside the weave.

Chhath Puja cleaning tips for the rest of the house

After the kitchen, focus on the rooms and spaces used during the festival. Sweep and wash the entrance and courtyard, since relatives often visit across the four days. Clean the roof or terrace if you will dry grain there or use it for an offering, clearing dry leaves and bird droppings. Wash the bathrooms properly, because the observer bathes before the rituals and guests will use them too. Change bedsheets in the room where the person fasting will rest.

Ants, cockroaches and mosquitoes

Jaggery, ghee and fruit all draw pests, and a festival kitchen is busy late at night. Ants are the most common complaint, and a trail along the kitchen wall usually leads to a nest a home spray will not reach; our ant control treatment targets the colony itself. Cockroaches come out at night near the sink and gas stove, and gel baiting through cockroach control works best when done a week before cooking starts, so treated areas are not near prasad.

Mid-November is still mosquito season across north India. Empty coolers, flower pots and buckets, and get mosquito control done before the festival if your building has standing water nearby. Anyone who develops a fever after mosquito bites should see a doctor rather than wait it out.

Ghat and rooftop arghya hygiene

If you are looking for Chhath ghat cleaning services, public ghats are cleaned and managed by municipal bodies and local committees, so check your local administration for advisories on which ghats are open. For your own family, pack clean towels, a full change of clothes, drinking water and covered containers for prasad. Carry a bag for waste and bring it home, and keep plastic out of the water. Ghat steps are slippery at dawn, so wear footwear with grip until you reach the water.

Families who make the offering on a rooftop or in a society often use a clean tub or a temporary pool. Scrub it with detergent and rinse it well a day before, fill it with clean water on the day, and keep it covered until use. If the water comes from your overhead tank, make sure the tank itself is clean; our water tank cleaning in Delhi NCR includes emptying, scrubbing and disinfecting the tank. After the dawn offering, empty the tub the same day and stand it upside down to dry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do Chhath cleaning on the first day of the festival?

It is better not to. The first day already involves bathing, a special meal and preparation, and wet floors or fresh polish need hours to dry. Finish the kitchen and prasad area at least a day before, and limit the festival days to quick wipes after each cooking session.

Which cleaners are safe to use in a prasad kitchen?

Mild dish soap, baking soda and plain hot water handle almost everything in a prasad kitchen. Rinse surfaces with clean water after any cleaner so no residue touches food or utensils. Avoid strong-smelling sprays near the cooking area and never mix bleach with ammonia or acid cleaners.

How long does thekua stay fresh?

Because it is fried and dry, thekua usually keeps for several days in a clean, dry, airtight tin at room temperature. Let it cool fully before storing and keep the tin away from the stove and damp areas. If you notice a sour or stale smell, or any mould, throw it away.

Is pest control safe a few days before cooking prasad?

Gel baits for cockroaches and targeted ant treatment are placed in cracks and behind fittings, away from food surfaces. Book the visit about a week before cooking starts, keep utensils covered, and wipe counters with clean water afterwards. Ask the technician which products are used and follow the label on anything left behind.

Do you clean public Chhath ghats?

No. Public ghats are cleaned and managed by municipal bodies and local committees. We handle the work inside your home: the kitchen, bathrooms, roof or terrace, and a full deep clean, plus tank cleaning if your offering water comes from an overhead tank.
