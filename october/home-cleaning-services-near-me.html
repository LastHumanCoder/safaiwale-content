How often should you book a professional clean?

There is no single right interval, but a few patterns hold for most Indian homes. A flat with a daily maid usually needs a professional full clean every few months, because fans, high shelves, grills and kitchen tiles collect grease and dust that daily mopping never reaches. Homes that cook with a lot of tadka and frying need the kitchen done more often than the rest of the house. Book an extra clean at these points:

  • After the monsoon, when damp corners, window channels and balconies have collected mould and silt.
  • Two to three weeks before Diwali, so the crew slot is confirmed before the festival rush.
  • Before moving in or after moving out, when the home is empty and every cabinet can be reached.
  • After pest control, once the technician says it is fine to wipe the treated areas.

What should you check before booking?

The best home cleaning service is the one whose quote matches what turns up on the day. Ask these questions before you pay:

  • Is the price fixed? Ask whether the rate depends on home size or on hours. Hourly billing can grow if the crew works slowly.
  • What is included? Check whether the chimney, fans, windows and balconies are covered, or charged extra.
  • Who brings the materials? A professional crew brings its own machines and cleaning agents.
  • Which chemicals are used? Ask for products suited to marble, wood and stone, and mention children or pets.
  • What if something is missed? A clear redo policy matters more than a polished website.

We recommend that you or someone you trust is at home at the start, to show the crew around, and at the end, to walk through the rooms. You do not need to stay in each room while the crew works. Lock away cash, jewellery and documents before the visit as a general precaution.

Do you clean inside kitchen cabinets and the fridge?

The standard full home clean covers cabinet fronts, tiles, hob and counters. Cabinet interiors and the inside of the fridge or oven need emptying first, so mention them when you request the quote and we will confirm whether they are included for your home and how long they add.

Can I book home cleaning on a weekend or a holiday?

Yes, weekend slots are available in the cities we serve, and they fill quickly before festivals such as Diwali. Book a week or more ahead for Saturdays and Sundays in October and early November, and share any building entry rules so the crew can get in on time.

The crew uses professional cleaning agents matched to the surface: degreasers for kitchens, descalers for bathroom fittings and milder products for wood, marble and painted walls.
