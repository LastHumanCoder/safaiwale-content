New flat, resale flat or wedding home: what changes

A brand-new flat

The main enemy is fine construction dust. It settles inside cabinets, on top of door frames and in window tracks, and it keeps reappearing for days if the first clean only mops the floor. Expect paint flecks on tiles and switchboards, cement haze on bathroom walls, and protective stickers on windows and fittings that leave glue behind. The team works top down, ceiling fans to floor, so dust falls once and gets cleaned once.

A resale or rented flat

Here the dirt is the previous occupant's: kitchen grease on cabinet doors and the wall behind the hob, hard-water scale on taps and shower glass, and dust inside wardrobes. A resale flat also carries a higher chance of cockroaches and bed bugs, which is why the pest treatment matters more here.

Your own home before a wedding

A wedding home is furnished and lived in, so the clean shifts towards fabrics and guest areas: sofas, curtains, mattresses, carpets and extra bathrooms. That is the subject of the next section.

Pre wedding home deep cleaning priorities

Wedding season home cleaning services get booked around function dates, and in north India the season usually picks up in the second half of November. With relatives arriving and functions at home, put your effort where guests will notice it:

  1. Guest bathrooms. Scale on taps and stained grout show first. A single bathroom deep clean is ₹499.
  2. Sofas and seating. Every visitor sits here. Book sofa cleaning a week ahead so the fabric dries fully.
  3. Mattresses in guest rooms. Spare mattresses that have been in storage collect dust mites and odour; mattress cleaning sorts that before relatives arrive.
  4. Carpets and rugs. Clean them before the functions, then roll up the good ones during mehendi and haldi.
  5. Terrace or hall for functions. Clear and wash it before the tent or decorators arrive.

Haldi stains need quick action. Wipe turmeric off marble and tiles within the hour with dish soap and water; left overnight, it sets into the stone. Turmeric on light fabrics fades faster if the washed cloth is dried in direct sun. Put up lights and drapes after the deep clean, not before, or the team has to work around them.

When to book the clean

For a new home, the sequence is what matters. Finish painting, carpentry and electrical work, then the deep clean, then pest control, then the ceremony and the move. Cleaning first matters because construction dust and paint residue stop gel baits sticking and dilute sprays. Leave at least a day between the clean and the treatment. Move in cleaning checklist for India

The cleaning team handles the surfaces. This new home cleaning checklist covers what you should check yourself in the same week, so problems surface before your furniture hides them:

  1. Make sure water supply and electricity are on before the cleaners arrive. Deep cleaning a flat without running water is not possible.
  2. Open the overhead tank lid and look inside. Builder tanks often hold sludge from construction; book water tank cleaning before you drink or cook with that water.
  3. Run every tap and flush each WC for a minute to clear stagnant water and spot leaks.
  4. Pour a bucket of water into each floor drain and check that it drains quickly without a smell.
  5. Switch on every fan, light, exhaust fan and geyser once, while the rooms are empty and faults are easy to report.
  6. Look under the kitchen sink and behind the cabinets for droppings, egg cases or dead cockroaches.
  7. Check door and window frames for small holes, fine powder or mud lines, which can signal termites.
  8. Line wardrobe and kitchen shelves only after the deep clean, never before.
  9. Keep a small kit for the first week: microfibre cloths, a broom, a mop, dish soap and bin liners.

Housewarming deep cleaning checklist for the day before

On the eve of the ceremony, keep it light: mop the floors once, clear the kitchen counter for cooking, stock bathrooms with soap and hand towels, set out a shoe rack at the entrance and put lined bins in the kitchen and each bathroom. What to ask before you book

  • Is the price fixed? Ask for the full amount in writing, by home size, before the visit.
  • What exactly is included? Check whether cabinet interiors, window tracks, grills and balcony are covered, or charged extra.
  • Who brings the equipment? A proper deep clean needs scrubbing machines, ladders and vacuum units, not just mops.
  • How long will it take? Plan for most of a working day for a 2 or 3 BHK, longer for a villa, and make sure someone can let the team in.
  • Can pest control follow? One provider for both makes the cleaning and treatment dates easier to line up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days before Griha Pravesh should I get the house cleaned?

Two to four days before is ideal. That leaves time for pest control after the clean and for any missed spots to be fixed, while still being close enough that dust does not settle again. Make sure all painting and carpentry is finished first, or the clean will need repeating.

Can I do the ceremony and move in on the same day as the deep clean?

It is possible but tight. A full deep clean of a 2 or 3 BHK takes most of a working day, and freshly mopped floors and washed bathrooms need time to dry. Booking the clean at least a day or two earlier gives you a calm ceremony day.

Do you remove construction debris?

No. A deep clean covers dust, paint marks, cement haze and grime, but bulk debris such as broken tiles, cement bags and wood offcuts should be removed by the builder or contractor before the team arrives. Clearing it first also lets the team reach every corner of the floor.

Is the new flat deep clean priced higher because of construction dust?

No. We charge the same fixed full-home rate by home size, so a new 3 BHK is ₹6,000 before the online discount whether it has construction dust or not. The job may take a little longer, but the price follows the size of your home.

Should I clean the water tank in a brand-new building?

Yes. Overhead and underground tanks in new buildings often hold sediment and debris from construction, and they may have sat with stagnant water for weeks. Get the tank emptied, scrubbed and disinfected before you use the water for drinking or cooking, and treat large or underground tanks as a job for professionals.
