Dhanteras Cleaning Tips: What to Clean Before Diwali Week

The most useful Dhanteras cleaning tips are about timing: finish the heavy cleaning in the week after Karwa Chauth (Thursday 29 October 2026), so that Dhanteras on Friday 6 November is left for the entrance, the puja shelf and polishing the metal you will use. Most Dhanteras cleaning tips stop at "clean the whole house", which does not tell you what to do on which day. These Dhanteras cleaning tips go occasion by occasion, from Karwa Chauth through Diwali on Sunday 8 November to Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj, so each day has one clear job. Follow these Dhanteras cleaning tips and your home is ready before Diwali week begins, not halfway through it.

Why do families clean the house before Dhanteras?

Dhanteras opens the Diwali festival in much of India, and many families believe a clean, well lit home welcomes Goddess Lakshmi, which is the heart of the Dhanteras home cleaning significance. There is a practical side too. Dhanteras is a traditional day to buy gold, silver, brass or steel utensils, so cleaning before Dhanteras shopping clears space for what comes home. Finishing early also means you are not scrubbing a bathroom on the evening you want to light diyas.

Karwa Chauth home preparation tips (Thursday 29 October)

Many women fast through the day on Karwa Chauth, so keep the cleaning short and do it in the morning. Two places matter most.

The balcony or terrace

Many families step out to the terrace or balcony in the evening to see the moon, so sweep it, wipe the railing and replace any fused bulb. If pigeons have left droppings, wear gloves and a mask, spray the droppings with water first so dry dust does not rise, and scrape them into a bag. If the birds keep returning, our guide on getting rid of pigeons from balconies and roofs explains the options.

The puja shelf and the items you will use

Dust the shelf, wash the cloth beneath the idols, and wash and fully dry the thali and any brass or steel items for the day. That is enough. The deep work starts the next morning.

What should you clean in the week before Dhanteras?

This is when the real house cleaning before Dhanteras happens. Put the heaviest jobs on the weekend of 31 October and 1 November, when you have daylight and time.

Kitchen first

Start with the tiles behind the stove, the chimney filter and the gas burners, since festive cooking starts within days. Soak burners in hot water with dish soap and clear the holes with a pin. If you see cockroaches while emptying cabinets, treat them before you deep clean; our cockroach control service does a gel treatment that stays effective through the festival.

Bathrooms and lofts

Descale taps and showerheads, scrub grout and wash the bathroom mats. Bring down whatever is in the loft, wipe the boxes and send what you do not need to the kabadiwala. Our bathroom cleaning tips go into tile and fitting care in detail.

Make room before Dhanteras shopping (Thursday 5 November)

Empty one kitchen shelf or a drawer in the almirah for what you will buy. New steel utensils usually carry a sticker and a light factory oil; soak them in warm soapy water, rub off the glue with a drop of cooking oil, then wash and dry them before first use.

What should you clean on Dhanteras itself?

Keep Dhanteras for the entrance and the metal. If the week went to plan, the rest of the house only needs a quick dust.

Main door and entrance

Wipe the main door on both sides, the handle, the nameplate and the bell switch. Scrub the threshold and the floor outside your door so the space is ready for rangoli or a toran. Replace the doormat if it no longer comes clean.

Brass, copper and silver

  • Brass and copper: rub with a paste of lemon juice and salt, or tamarind pulp, then rinse and dry fully so they do not spot. Lacquered pieces only need mild soap and water.
  • Silver: line a bowl with aluminium foil, add hot water and a spoon of baking soda, and dip the silver for a minute or two before rinsing and drying. Skip this for antique pieces, items with stones, or a deliberately darkened finish.
  • Gold jewellery: lukewarm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft toothbrush, then dry on a soft cloth.

Wear gloves for lemon and tamarind work if your hands are sensitive, and never mix bleach with acidic or ammonia based cleaners anywhere in the house.

What is left for Saturday 7 November, the day before Diwali?

Use this day for lighting and guests. Plug in each string of lights before you hang it and discard any with frayed wires or melted plugs. Spread the load across sockets instead of running every string from one multi plug. Clean the guest bathroom last, put out a fresh hand towel and soap, and keep curtains, paper decorations and rangoli powder well away from where diyas and candles will sit. A bucket of water or sand on the balcony is a sensible precaution for the evening.

Govardhan Puja cleaning tips (the day after Diwali)

Govardhan Puja is usually the day after Diwali, though some calendars place it a day later, so check your panchang. In many homes it is a big cooking day, so the kitchen needs a reset afterwards: soak heavy kadhais overnight, wipe the hob and tiles while the grease is still soft, and run hot water through the sink drain.

Diwali also leaves its own marks. Blot fresh diya oil on marble or stone, cover the spot with baking soda or talc, leave it overnight and sweep it up. For wax, harden it with an ice cube, lift it with a plastic card, and wipe the rest with warm water. Our step by step guide to marble floor care covers deeper stains. Sweep spent crackers off the balcony with gloves on, and soak any that did not burst in a bucket of water before you throw them away.

Bhai Dooj home preparation (a couple of days after Diwali)

Bhai Dooj usually falls a couple of days after Diwali; again, confirm the date locally. It often brings a second round of guests, so focus on the rooms they will use. Vacuum the sofa and shake out the cushions, wipe the dining table and chairs, clear the fridge of leftovers older than two days, and move the remaining sweets into airtight boxes. A 20 minute reset of the living room, dining area and guest bathroom is all most homes need if the earlier days were done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it fine to clean the house on Dhanteras day itself?

Yes, light cleaning on Dhanteras is common, such as wiping the entrance, the main door and the puja shelf. Customs differ between families, so follow what your household practises. From a practical view, keep the heavy scrubbing for the days before so you have time for shopping and evening preparations.

What should I buy before Dhanteras for cleaning?

Stock up a week ahead, as shops get crowded near the festival. A useful list is microfibre cloths, rubber gloves, dish soap, baking soda, white vinegar, lemons, a stiff brush, a floor cleaner and a new doormat. Read the label of any chemical cleaner and keep it away from children.

How do I clean brass diyas that have black oil stains?

Soak the diyas in hot water with dish soap for about 30 minutes to soften the burnt oil, then scrub with a pinch of salt on a soft brush. Finish with a lemon and salt paste for shine, rinse well and dry with a cloth. Avoid steel wool, which scratches brass.

Can I do pest control in Diwali week?

It is better done two to three weeks before Dhanteras, so the treatment has time to work and any smell has cleared before guests arrive. Pest control should also come before deep cleaning, or the cleaners will wipe the fresh treatment away. If you see cockroaches in festival week, a gel treatment in the kitchen is the least disruptive option.

How do I keep the house clean during the five days of Diwali?

Spend 10 minutes each morning on the entrance, the kitchen counter and the guest bathroom, and empty the bins every night, since sweet boxes and food waste attract ants quickly. Keep a cloth and a small bowl of soapy water near the puja area for oil drips. That small daily reset stops the mess from building up across the week.
