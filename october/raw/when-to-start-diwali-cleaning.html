When to Start Diwali Cleaning: A 10-Day Plan for Busy People

If you are deciding when to start Diwali cleaning, start about ten days before Dhanteras: in 2026 that means a plan running from Monday 26 October to Wednesday 4 November, with 20 to 30 minutes of work each day. Working people often ask when to start Diwali cleaning because one marathon weekend leaves them exhausted and the home only half done. The practical answer to when to start Diwali cleaning is early enough that every job gets its own day, and late enough that dust does not settle again before Diwali on Sunday 8 November. Below is the day by day plan, plus what to book earlier and what to hand over, so you know exactly when to start Diwali cleaning in your own home.

When should you start Diwali cleaning in 2026?

Think of it in three stages. Around three weeks before Diwali (the weekend of 17 and 18 October), declutter, buy supplies and book anything that needs a technician, such as pest control or a professional deep clean. From Monday 26 October, run the 10 day plan below, one short task each evening. Keep Thursday 5 November as a spare day for anything that slipped, then use Dhanteras on Friday 6 November and Saturday 7 November for shopping, rangoli and lights.

Two rules shape this Diwali house cleaning schedule. Clean top to bottom, so dust from fans and shelves lands on floors you have not mopped yet. And clean the floors last, on Day 10, so the house is at its freshest when guests arrive.

Days 1 to 3: fans, windows and the kitchen

Day 1: ceiling fans and light fittings

Switch the fan off at the switchboard and wait until it stops completely. Slide an old pillow cover over each blade and pull it back, so the dust stays inside the cover instead of falling on you. Wipe tube lights and the tops of wardrobes with a damp microfibre cloth. Use a stable step ladder, not a stool on a bed.

Day 2: windows, grills and sliding tracks

Vacuum the sliding tracks first with a crevice nozzle, then scrub them with an old toothbrush and soapy water. Wipe glass with a mix of water and a little dish soap, then dry it with crumpled newspaper or a dry microfibre cloth for a streak free finish. If you only get through half the windows, finish the rest on the spare day.

Day 3: kitchen cabinet fronts and the chimney filter

Soak the chimney filter overnight in hot water with baking soda and dish soap, after checking your manual for what the filter can take. While it soaks, wipe cabinet fronts and handles, where grease builds up most. For more on stubborn kitchen grime, our kitchen cleaning tips go stain by stain.

Days 4 to 6: puja corner, fridge and bathrooms

Day 4: the puja corner, on Karwa Chauth

Many women fast on Karwa Chauth, so this is the lightest day in the plan. Dust the shelf, wash the cloth beneath the idols, and clean brass diyas and the thali with a paste of lemon juice and salt, then rinse and dry them fully so they do not spot.

Day 5: fridge and microwave

Empty one shelf at a time so food is never out for long. Wipe shelves with warm water and a mild detergent. For the microwave, heat a bowl of water with a few lemon slices for about three minutes, leave the door shut for a minute, and the dried splashes wipe off easily.

Day 6: bathrooms

Spray tiles, leave the cleaner on for ten minutes, and scrub grout with a stiff brush. Wrap taps in a cloth soaked in white vinegar to loosen hard water marks, but keep vinegar away from marble, which it etches. Never mix bleach with ammonia or acid based cleaners, and keep the exhaust fan running or the window open while you work.

Days 7 to 10: fabrics, bedrooms, entrance and floors

Day 7: curtains, cushion covers and the sofa

Sunday is laundry day for the plan. Put curtains and cushion covers in the machine, and while it runs, vacuum the sofa with the upholstery nozzle, including under the cushions. Dry clean silk and heavy velvet curtains rather than washing them.

Day 8: bedrooms

Change to fresh bedsheets, vacuum the mattress, flip or rotate it, and wipe the headboard and bedside tables. Vacuum under the bed while the room is clear.

Day 9: main door, entrance and balcony

Wipe the main door, the nameplate and the bell. Scrub the doormat or replace it. Sweep the balcony, wipe the railings and clear dried leaves from the drain outlet.

Day 10: floors and the final walk through

Sweep or vacuum every room, then mop from the far end of the house towards the door. Walk through with a bag and pick up anything out of place. Our guide to floor care for a lasting shine covers marble, tiles and wood.

How do you fit Diwali cleaning around a full time job?

These Diwali cleaning tips for working people come down to removing every small delay. Keep one bucket ready with gloves, microfibre cloths, a scrub brush, baking soda, dish soap and a spray bottle, so you start the moment you get home. Set a 25 minute timer and stop when it rings. Pick a fixed slot, such as right after dinner, when the kitchen is already being cleaned.

A few Diwali cleaning hacks save real time. Let soaking do the hard work: the chimney filter, gas stove burners and oily jars clean far faster after a night in hot soapy water. Clean glass on a cloudy evening, as sun dries the liquid before you can wipe it. Split days between family members; a teenager can take Day 2 while you take Day 3. Cleaning in short daily blocks is how you get Diwali cleaning without stress, because no single evening is heavy and nothing waits for the last weekend. How should small homes adjust the plan?

These Diwali cleaning ideas for small homes shorten the plan rather than cut corners. In a studio or 1 BHK, combine Days 1 and 2, Days 5 and 8, and Days 9 and 10, which gives you a 7 day plan you can start as late as Thursday 29 October. If you are fasting that day, do the light puja corner task first and shift the rest by one. In a small kitchen, empty only the upper cabinets one evening and the lower cabinets the next, so the counter never disappears under jars. For a 3 BHK or a home with two balconies, add the spare day on 5 November to the plan.

Which jobs are worth booking with professionals?

Some jobs do not fit into 30 minutes: degreasing a heavily used kitchen, scrubbing bathroom tiles that have yellowed, shampooing a fabric sofa, or treating cockroaches. Book these two to three weeks before Diwali, as slots fill up near the festival. If you are also booking pest control, schedule it before any deep cleaning so the cleaners do not wipe away fresh treatment. Frequently Asked Questions

Is it too late to start Diwali cleaning one week before the festival?

No, but you will need to double up. Pair two tasks from the plan each evening for five days, and hand the heaviest jobs, usually the kitchen and bathrooms, to a professional team. Book that team immediately, as slots in the last week before Diwali go quickly.

Should I clean on the day of Dhanteras?

Keep Dhanteras for light work only, such as wiping the entrance, the main door and the shelf where new purchases will go. If the 10 day plan finished on 4 November, the heavy cleaning is already done. Many families prefer to spend the day shopping and preparing rather than scrubbing.

What cleaning supplies do I need for the 10 day plan?

A bucket, rubber gloves, four or five microfibre cloths, a stiff scrub brush, an old toothbrush, baking soda, white vinegar, dish soap, a floor cleaner and a spray bottle cover almost every task. Add a step ladder and a vacuum with a crevice nozzle if you have one. Read the label on any cleaner before use and never mix bleach with other products.

How long does a professional deep clean take compared with this plan?

A professional team usually finishes a 2 BHK deep clean in a single day, with several people working together and machines for scrubbing and vacuuming. The plan here spreads the same kind of work over ten evenings for one person. Many families combine the two: professionals for the kitchen and bathrooms, and the daily plan for everything else.

Can I follow this plan if I live alone in a rented flat?

Yes, and a studio or 1 BHK suits the shorter 7 day version described above. Check with your landlord before using strong cleaners on fittings, and photograph any existing damage before you start. Skip tasks that do not apply, such as a chimney filter if your kitchen has none.
