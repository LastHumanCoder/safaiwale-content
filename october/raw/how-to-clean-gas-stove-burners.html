How to Clean Gas Stove Burners Before Diwali: 8 Easy Ways

The short answer to how to clean gas stove burners: turn off the gas, let the burners cool, soak them in hot soapy water or a baking soda or vinegar solution, scrub the flame holes with an old toothbrush, then dry them completely before you relight. That covers most kitchens, but how to clean gas stove burners well depends on how thick the black crust is and whether your burners are brass or aluminium. Below are 8 methods for how to clean gas stove burners, from a weekly soak to clearing blocked flame ports, with a table to help you pick. Once you know how to clean gas stove burners, the same pre-Diwali session can take in the chimney, cabinets and sink, which we cover in short sections after the burners.

Gas safety before you start

  • Turn off the supply. Close every burner knob, then switch off the cylinder regulator, or the main valve if you have piped gas.
  • Let it cool. Burners stay hot for a while after cooking. Wait at least 20 minutes before you lift them off.
  • Keep liquid away from knobs and ignition. Wipe around knobs and the auto-ignition point with a damp cloth; never pour or spray cleaner over them.
  • Dry burners fully before relighting. Water trapped in the flame holes gives a weak, yellow or uneven flame and can stop the burner lighting. Towel-dry, then leave them in the air for two hours or in sunlight.
  • Refit and test. Seat each burner cap flat on its base, open the regulator and check for an even blue flame.

If you ever smell gas, do not touch electrical switches or light anything. Close the regulator, open doors and windows, and call 1906, the LPG emergency helpline, or your piped gas provider's emergency number. While the stove is apart, check the rubber hose for cracks or hardening, and replace a worn one with an ISI-marked hose.

8 easy ways to clean gas stove burners

Most Indian stoves have a burner head and a cap that lift off the mixing tube. Clean both parts, and wipe the pan supports and drip tray at the same time.

1. Hot water and dish soap soak

Fill a basin with water as hot as your gloves allow, add a good squeeze of dish soap and soak the burners for 30 minutes. Scrub every face and the flame holes with an old toothbrush, then rinse. This is the weekly method; it handles fresh spills and light grease on any burner.

2. Baking soda paste

Mix three spoons of baking soda with one spoon of water into a thick paste. Spread it over the brown, baked-on patches and leave it for 15 to 20 minutes, then scrub with a toothbrush or a soft scrub pad and rinse. Baking soda is mildly abrasive, so it lifts crust without scratching brass.

3. Vinegar soak, then baking soda

Soak the burners for 20 to 30 minutes in equal parts warm water and white vinegar. Lift them out, sprinkle baking soda over the wet surface and scrub as it fizzes. The vinegar softens grease and hard-water marks and the soda gives grip. Rinse well, because vinegar left on brass for hours can dull it.

4. Lemon and salt

For brass burners that have gone dark or patchy, cut a lemon in half, dip it in salt and rub it over the burner like a scrubber. Leave the juice for five minutes, then rinse and dry. Use it after a soak; it brightens rather than degreases.

5. Fruit salt and hot water

A sachet of fruit salt (the antacid powder) in a bowl of hot water fizzes strongly, because it combines a mild acid with baking soda. Drop the burners in for 20 to 30 minutes and the bubbling loosens grime in the holes. Scrub and rinse.

6. Tamarind pulp

Soak a small ball of tamarind in warm water for ten minutes, then rub the softened pulp over brass burners and leave it for 10 to 15 minutes. Tamarind is a mild natural acid and is a long-standing way to brighten brass in Indian kitchens. Rinse thoroughly so no sticky residue stays in the holes.

7. Washing soda boil (brass only)

For burners black with years of carbon, put them in an old steel pan of water with a spoon of washing soda and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes with the window open. Let the water cool before you take them out, then scrub. Wear gloves, because washing soda irritates skin. Do not use this on aluminium burners: strong alkali pits and darkens aluminium.

8. Clear blocked flame ports

If the flame is yellow, patchy or missing in places, the ports are blocked. After a soak, push a safety pin or thin wire straight into each hole and pull it out, without twisting or widening the hole. Avoid toothpicks, which snap off inside. Tap the burner upside down to shake debris loose and rinse. If the flame stays yellow once the burner is clean, dry and seated correctly, call the gas agency's mechanic.

How to deep clean a kitchen chimney

Switch the chimney off at the mains first. Remove the filters as your user manual shows; most slide or clip out from below.

  • Baffle and mesh filters: soak in the hottest water you can manage with dish soap and four spoons of baking soda for one to two hours. Scrub with a soft brush, never a wire brush on mesh, rinse and let them dry completely before refitting.
  • Charcoal filters in recirculating chimneys cannot be washed. Replace them on the schedule in the manual.
  • Oil collector: filterless and auto-clean models drain grease into a cup or tray. Empty and wash it, then run the auto-clean cycle as the manual describes.
  • Hood: spray degreaser onto a cloth, not onto the chimney, and wipe the outside panels and control buttons. Keep water away from the motor and wiring.

If you fry most days, wash baffle filters every few weeks rather than once a year. A thick oil layer on the filter cuts suction and can drip back onto the hob.

How to clean kitchen cabinets

Empty one cabinet at a time. Vacuum or brush out crumbs, wipe the shelves with warm water and a little dish soap, and dry them before anything goes back. Look in the corners for pepper-like droppings or egg cases; if you find them, book cockroach control after the clean, before you restock.

Doors need a method that suits the finish. Laminate takes a mild soap solution and a soft cloth. High-gloss acrylic and PU finishes scratch easily, so use microfibre only and nothing abrasive. Wood or veneer should be wiped with a cloth wrung almost dry and dried straight away. The sticky film on doors near the hob comes off with a paste of baking soda and a few drops of dish soap, rubbed gently and wiped clean. Finish with handles and hinges.

How to clean a stainless steel sink

  1. Rinse out food bits and clear the strainer.
  2. Sprinkle baking soda over the damp sink and scrub with a soft sponge, following the direction of the brushed grain.
  3. For white water spots, wipe with a cloth dampened in vinegar, then rinse within a few minutes.
  4. Pour a kettle of hot water down the drain to clear grease.
  5. Dry the sink with a cloth so new water marks do not form.

Skip steel wool, which leaves tiny particles that rust and stain the steel, and do not leave bleach sitting in the sink, as it can pit stainless steel over time. Never mix bleach with vinegar or ammonia-based cleaners; the fumes are toxic.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should gas stove burners be cleaned?

Give them a quick dish soap soak once a week if you cook daily, and a deeper clean with baking soda, vinegar or tamarind once a month. Wipe spills from the pan supports and drip tray after each meal, because boiled-over milk and dal harden into crust within a day.

Can I put gas burners in the dishwasher?

Check your stove manual first. Brass burners usually discolour in a dishwasher, and the strong detergent can dull aluminium. A hand soak in hot soapy water is gentler and just as effective, and it lets you check every flame hole as you scrub.

Why is my gas flame yellow after cleaning?

The most common causes are water still inside the ports, a burner cap that is not seated flat, or holes still partly blocked. Dry the burner for longer, reseat the cap and clear the ports with a pin. If the flame stays yellow, ask your gas agency to send a mechanic.

Is it safe to use oven cleaner or harsh degreaser on a glass-top stove?

Avoid harsh oven cleaners and abrasive powders on toughened glass tops, as they can scratch or cloud the surface. Use a soft cloth with dish soap, or a baking soda paste on stubborn marks, and wipe it dry. Always follow the label and your stove maker's care instructions.

How long should burners dry before I relight the stove?

Towel-dry them, then leave them in open air for at least two hours, or in direct sunlight, until no water remains in the flame holes. Shake each burner over a cloth; if any drops fall out, give it more time. Relighting a wet burner gives a poor flame and can stop it lighting.
