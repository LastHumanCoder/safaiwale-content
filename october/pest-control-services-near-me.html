What are the types of pest control services?

The types of pest control services fall into a few groups, and knowing them helps you ask for the right one instead of a vague "general spray".

General pest control

A routine treatment for crawling insects: cockroaches, ants, silverfish and spiders. Most homes book it once or twice a year. It does not cover termites or bed bugs, which need their own treatment. Our post on general pest control vs termite control explains the split.

Cockroach control

If you searched "cockroach control near me", you most likely have German cockroaches in the kitchen. Gel bait works better than spray alone here, because cockroaches carry it back to their hiding spots. See our cockroach control service for the full method.

Termite control

Termites eat wood from the inside, so the damage often shows late. Treatment for an occupied home involves drilling small holes along the wall base near affected wood and injecting termiticide to create a barrier. Details are on our termite control page.

Bed bug control

Bed bugs hide in seams and joints and survive a single spray as eggs, so this treatment always runs in two rounds. Our bed bug control service includes the follow-up.

Mosquito and rodent control

Mosquito control combines spraying with removing breeding water, and rodent control combines baiting with closing entry holes. If anyone in the home gets a fever after mosquito bites, see a doctor first; pest control helps prevent the next bite, it does not treat illness. See mosquito control and rat control.

Low-exposure options for sensitive homes

For homes with infants, elderly family members or pets, ask for gel and bait methods, which keep chemical exposure low in open living areas. Tell the technician about asthma or allergies at the inspection.

How does a pest control visit work?

  1. Free inspection. A technician checks the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, balconies and entry points, identifies the pest and judges how far it has spread.

How is the price for pest control decided?

We do not publish fixed pest control prices, because two homes of the same size can need very different work. The quote depends on:

  • The pest. A cockroach gel treatment and a termite drill-and-inject job use different products, tools and time.
  • How bad the infestation is. A few cockroaches under the sink is a short job; cockroaches in every room and inside appliances need more product and often a second round.
  • Home size and layout. More rooms, bathrooms and wooden fittings mean more area to treat.
  • Number of rounds. Bed bugs and severe cockroach problems need follow-up visits.
  • Warranty or annual plan. Ongoing protection with scheduled visits is priced differently from a one-time treatment.

What should you check before booking?

The best home pest control service is the one that gets the diagnosis right and is open about products and safety. Before you book, check these points with any provider.

  • Do they inspect first? A quote given over the phone without anyone seeing the home is a guess. Insist on an inspection.
  • Which product will they use? Ask for the product name. Insecticides sold in India must be registered with the Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee, and the label carries that registration and the safety instructions.
  • What is the exact scope? Rooms, pests and number of rounds should be in writing.
  • What are the safety instructions? You should be told how long to stay out, what to do with food and utensils, and how to protect children and pets.
  • What happens if pests return? Ask what the follow-up covers and within how many days you can call them back.
  • Who turns up? Confirm the technician is trained by the company you booked, not an unknown subcontractor.

A provider that answers all six clearly is worth more than a lower quote that leaves them vague. That is also how to judge the best pest control service, or the best pest control company in India, for your needs: by what it puts in writing, not by its advertising.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a one-time treatment enough, or do I need an annual plan?

A one-time treatment suits a light, recent problem such as a few cockroaches in the kitchen. An annual plan with scheduled visits suits homes that see pests every season, older buildings and ground floor flats. The technician will recommend one after the free inspection, and the choice stays yours.

Is pest control safe for children and pets?

It is safe when the product is used as its label directs and you follow the re-entry time the technician gives you. Keep children and pets out of treated rooms until then, cover aquariums, and move pet bowls and toys. Tell us about infants, pregnancy, asthma or pets at the inspection so we can pick gel and bait methods where possible.

Do I need to leave the house during treatment?

For gel bait treatments you usually do not need to leave, only to keep away from the areas being treated. For spray treatments the technician will give you a time to stay out of the treated rooms, often a few hours, and will tell you when it is fine to air the rooms and return.

Should I clean my kitchen before or after pest control?

Clean before the visit by clearing shelves and wiping away grease and crumbs, since food residue competes with bait. After treatment, do not wipe the cracks, hinges and corners where gel or spray was applied, as that removes the product. Wash utensils and counters you use for food as usual.

How often should a home get pest control?

It depends on the pest and the building. Many homes book general pest control once or twice a year, often after the monsoon, while termite and bed bug treatments follow their own schedule set at the inspection. Older buildings, ground floor flats and homes near drains or markets usually need it more often.
